The files derive from NIST Special Databases 1 and 3, United States
government works. LeCun's original distribution attaches no explicit license
text. The CC BY-SA 3.0 designation carried in this directory is the
conservative attribution used by the canonical public mirrors (Kaggle and
the tf/keras distribution metadata); we record it rather than assert a
weaker one ourselves.
