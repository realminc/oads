PatchCamelyon benchmark: 327,680 color patches (96x96) extracted from
Camelyon16 lymph-node histopathology scans, binary tumor-present labels.
camelyonpatch_level_2_split_{train,valid,test}_{x,y}.h5.gz hold uint8
HWC image datasets `/x` and label datasets `/y`; split meta CSVs record
source-slide provenance and are redistributed unmodified.
