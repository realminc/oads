NSynth test split (nsynth-test.jsonwav.tar.gz)

Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/magentadata/datasets/nsynth/
License: CC-BY-4.0
Citation: Engel et al., "Neural Audio Synthesis of Musical Notes
with WaveNet Autoencoders", ICML 2017.

Only the test split (4,096 notes, 16,000 Hz mono PCM, four-second
clips) is distributed through this pack. `examples.json` per-note
metadata (pitch, velocity, instrument id, family, qualities) joins
`audio/<note_str>.wav` members at index time.
