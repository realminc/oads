Stanford Cars-196 (cars196_train.zip + cars196_test.zip)

Source mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/haodoz0118/cars196-img
Original: Stanford AI Lab (Krause et al., "3D Object
Representations for Fine-Grained Categorization", 3DRR 2013);
ai.stanford.edu/~jkrause/cars/car_dataset.html is retired.

The upstream distribution attaches no license text. Images were
collected from public web sources for research benchmarking;
redistribution terms are unspecified. The loaded mirror files ship
flat `NNNNN_<Make>_<Model>_<Year>.jpg` members rather than the
original MATLAB-annotated layout; the class label derives from the
filename stem minus its leading ordinal.
