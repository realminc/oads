Fashion-MNIST clothing articles

Source: http://fashion-mnist.s3-website.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/
(train/t10k IDX ubyte payloads)
License: MIT (Zalando SE fashion-mnist repository)
Citation: Xiao, Rasul & Vollgraf, "Fashion-MNIST: a Novel Image
Dataset for Benchmarking Machine Learning Algorithms", 2017.

Drop-in MNIST replacement: identical IDX wire format, 60,000 train and
10,000 test `[1, 28, 28]` samples over 10 clothing classes.
