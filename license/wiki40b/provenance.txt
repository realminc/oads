Wiki-40B multilingual cleaned Wikipedia corpus (LREC 2020). The en config
carries train/validation/test TFRecord shards with `text` and
`wikidata_id` features; article text retains upstream
_START_ARTICLE_/_START_PARAGRAPH_/_NEWLINE_ markers. Disambiguation,
redirect, deleted, and non-entity pages are filtered upstream.
