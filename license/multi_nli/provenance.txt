MultiNLI 1.0 (multinli_1.0.zip)

Source: https://cims.nyu.edu/~sbowman/multinli/
Williams, Nangia, Bowman, "A Broad-Coverage Challenge Corpus for
Sentence Understanding through Inference", NAACL 2018. The corpus mixes
per-genre terms (OANC, various Creative Commons, and others); the zip
bundles its per-genre license notices. Rows whose annotators could not
agree drop out of this pack.
