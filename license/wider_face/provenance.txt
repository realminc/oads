WIDER FACE (WIDER_{train,val,test}.zip + wider_face_split.zip)

Source mirror: https://huggingface.co/datasets/CUHK-CSE/wider_face
Original: Yang et al., "WIDER FACE: A Face Detection Benchmark",
CVPR 2016, CUHK (wider-face.challenges). The dataset is distributed
for non-commercial research purposes; images originate from the
WIDER dataset (CUHK) and keep their upstream usage terms. The HF
mirror carries identical zip payloads. Test ground truth is not
public; the test split ships a filelist only.
