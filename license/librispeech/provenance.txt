LibriSpeech dev-clean (dev-clean.tar.gz)

Source: https://www.openslr.org/resources/12/dev-clean.tar.gz
License: CC-BY-4.0 (LibriVox public-domain narrations)
Citation: Panayotov et al., "Librispeech: An ASR Corpus Based on
Public Domain Audio Books", ICASSP 2015.

Only the dev-clean subset (2,703 utterances, 40 speakers, ~5.4h) is
distributed through this pack. `*.trans.txt` transcripts join
`<speaker>-<chapter>-<n>.flac` clips at index time; speaker ids derive
from the directory prefix, not from the upstream SPEAKERS.TXT table.
