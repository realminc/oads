Omniglot handwritten characters

Source: https://github.com/brendenlake/omniglot (images_background.zip,
images_evaluation.zip)
License: MIT (Lake et al. omniglot repository)
Citation: Lake, Salakhutdinov & Tenenbaum, "Human-level concept
learning through probabilistic program induction", Science 2015.

Classes are `<Alphabet>/<character>` directory pairs; character names
repeat across alphabets, so the alphabet name stays embedded in each
class label. `images_background` maps to train (19,280 images, 964
classes) and `images_evaluation` to test (13,180 images, 659 classes).
